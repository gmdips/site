What is GDIPS?
GDIPS (Freedom Geometry Dash Private Server) is a server that promises freedom — create, share and play without strict limitations.
Is it free?
Yes, it's completely free to play.
Is there an iOS version?
For the time being, use GDPS Switcher on Geode.

Tentang kami

GDIPS was developed by two people who wanted to create a free-to-express Geometry Dash server.
