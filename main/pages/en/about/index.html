GDIPS is a Geometry Dash Private Server (GDPS) developed by Fless with a spirit of freedom, creativity and community. On FreedomGDPS you can:

  • Create and share your own unique levels without strict limitations.
  • Access cool features such as UploadSong, Gauntlet, DemonList and much more!
  • Join an active community through Discord and our social media.
  • Take part in events, rates and community competitions.

GDIPS was built out of the wish to provide a free space for Geometry Dash creators, without being bound to the official platform rules. Anyone can become something here!
